How I get there

The house shows who I am in 2031. This kolam shows how I get there. Donella Meadows named twelve places to intervene in a system, from the easiest to change (numbers, on the outer ring) to the most powerful (paradigms, at the center). Each petal is one of three changes I want to make, drawn through all twelve.

Tap any cell. Arrow keys move between rings and petals.

The outer rings are where I start. The center is why it lasts.
